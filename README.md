# Monsternight

A small online party game made for a group of friends. It isn't commercial and it isn't serious.

The theme is classic horror, but it's played for fun, not for scares. Players move around a lively, Halloween-themed board by rolling dice and landing on spaces. Along the way they play short minigames: simple, well-known games like Pong or Haxball with a monster makeover.

## Players

- 2 to 8 players. Usually 4 or 5.
- Everyone runs their own copy of the game. One player hosts and the others join. Anyone can be the host.
- It's built to be played online across different cities in Turkey.
