# Questionnaire 05: Trophies and Duels

## 1. What happens once you reach the end of the board?

Let's say 12 turns, fixed. Once you reach the end, you sit out and watch. No trophies for third and more.

## 2. How do duel stakes work?

You can challenge anyone. For one drop of blood or one coin.  For the handicap, let's just say 3 rounds for whatever game. If the challenged player only has 1 health, they cannot be dueled for a death match unless they have no gold.

## 3. How do blood and death work?

Can't heal above 3. You don't keep your coins. You come back with full blood.
