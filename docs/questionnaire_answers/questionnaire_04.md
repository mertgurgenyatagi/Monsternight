# Questionnaire 04: Blood and Gold

## 1. Is the winner simply the first player to reach the end of the board?

Maybe we can go for a trophy thing.

Reaching the end first gives you a trophy (or two, with coming second giving one trophy). Having the most gold gives you a trophy. At the end, all the trophies are counted up to determine the leaderboard. There is a turn limit, that determines when the game ends.

## 2. What are coins for?

A suggestion above.

## 3. Should there be minigames besides duels?

Nah there's no free for all. Only 1v1.

## 4. Which of these spaces should be on the board?

Coin: you get a single coin
Spikes: you lose one HP
Thief: you lose a single coin
Church: you gain one HP
Pong: you duel someone at pong
Haxball: you play someone at haxball
(and many other minigames, with their own designated stops)

Also, there is another option for minigames besides gold and hp, for swapping places. However, you are severely handicapped in some way.

## 5. Does this art direction sound right?

It actually doesn't need to have a really dark mood. It can be hellishly colorful and animated. I don't want it to be bleak.
